Words you'll hear

The short glossary.

Escrow

The neutral company that holds the money and papers until the sale is done.

Earnest money

The buyer's deposit. It shows they're serious and counts toward the purchase.

Contingency

A condition the sale depends on. If it isn't met, the buyer can usually cancel.

Appraisal

The bank's check that the house is worth the price.

Title

Proof of who owns the house. Title insurance protects against old claims on it.

Recording

The county officially writing down the new owner. The moment it closes.
