Questions about price

The short answers.

Do I need a call to get started?

No. You can open a file by sending a text. We make everything easy for you: we have our own systems, and they snap right into place around the way you work. You don't have to worry about this part anymore. The only call we ask for is a planning call if you're doing 10 or more files a month.

What counts as more than 10 a month?

We're flexible. If you averaged more than 10 files a month over the last 3 months, or over the last 12 months, or in the previous calendar year, you qualify. We ask because we love working with high-volume agents, and we need to staff appropriately for you.

Is there a contract or a minimum?

No contract and no minimum. You pay per file you send.
